Spotify Distributor Connector

Panel origin

Loopback HTTP (127.0.0.1 / localhost) or HTTPS only. No path.

Pairing

In the panel, open the connector section and click “Generate pairing code”, then enter it here.

Status: —

Automatic capture

This connector never reads or sends Spotify tokens, cookies, headers, or account data. The connector key only authorizes delivery to your panel; it is not a Spotify token.
